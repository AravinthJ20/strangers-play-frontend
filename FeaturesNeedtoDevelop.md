To stand out, focus on features that solve specific problems rather than just adding more chat functionality.

If your app is a public social chat app where people connect, discover others, and chat (similar to WhatsApp + Telegram + Instagram DM + Discord), then features like ERP workflows won't help. You need features that make people want to stay in the app every day.

What makes a social chat app successful?

People stay for one or more of these:

Friends & relationships
Entertainment
Communities
Content creation
Discovery of new people
Features that can differentiate your app
